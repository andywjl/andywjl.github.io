About Me

I am an analyst at China Development Bank with expertise in corporate banking and development finance. My work bridges international policy analysis with practical financial operations.

I hold graduate degrees from The Johns Hopkins University School of Advanced International Studies (SAIS), including a Master of Arts in European Public Policy. My academic training has given me deep expertise in international economics, geopolitics, and public policy.

I have gained valuable international experience through programs such as the Carnegie-Tsinghua Center for Global Policy Young Ambassadors Program and an internship at the Carnegie Middle East Center in Beirut, Lebanon.
